Retirement withdrawal simulator Once you reach your target, how long will it last?

YearStarting balanceWithdrawal Lump sumGrowthEnding balance

The withdrawal starts at your chosen rate applied to the corpus at retirement, then rises with assumed inflation each year after (the standard "4% rule" shape) — not a fixed percentage of the shrinking or growing balance each year. This is a projection assuming steady growth and inflation, not a guarantee.

"Assumed annual return" defaults to each fund's XIRR-to-date, but you can type a different number to test a scenario. "Contribute for (yrs)" stops new contributions to that fund after that many years — leave it blank to keep contributing indefinitely (until the target is hit or the 50-year cap). "Step-up %/yr" raises that fund's contribution by this percentage at the start of every following year (a common step-up SIP pattern) — leave at 0 for a flat amount. Yearly contributions are spread evenly across the year for this projection. This is a straight-line projection off past performance, not a guarantee — real markets don't compound smoothly.

How much should I invest monthly? Reverse calculator — target amount, timeframe, and return rate → required SIP

Assumes you invest at the end of each month and the return compounds monthly at the rate you enter — the same convention the Goal Planner above uses, so the two stay consistent with each other. Change the return rate to see how sensitive the required monthly amount is to your assumption.
